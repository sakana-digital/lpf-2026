# Stalls: Send your status

Your stall reports two things — 販売状況 (sales) and 混雑状況 (queue) — and they appear on the [Explore](https://happo-sai.pages.dev/explore/events/) page of the public site and on the signage displays around school. Use it from a phone browser.

::: tip The app is in Japanese
There is no English UI. Japanese labels are quoted below exactly as they appear on screen, with a translation next to them.
:::

## 1. Open your access URL

Open the URL you were given (`https://…/?t=…`). Any number of people can be signed in at once.

- Opening it once saves the sign-in to that device.
- It is saved in the browser, so in these cases you have to start over. Open the URL you were given again.
  - You opened it in a private window.
  - You cleared the browser history or site data.
  - You are on a different device, or a different browser.
- Every stall has its own URL. **Do not show or share it with anyone else** — nobody should be changing another stall's status.

You are ready once your stall's name appears in the top right of the screen. The colours follow the device's light / dark setting.

## 2. Pick a status and send it

Pick one 販売状況 (sales) and one 混雑状況 (queue).

![The screen after signing in as 3年次9組: the stall name in the top right, with the sales and queue options below it.](/status-org.png)

- Send again whenever the situation changes.
- Choosing 販売休止中 (paused) or 全て完売 (sold out) disables the queue options.
- 全て完売 (sold out) asks you to confirm when you send. **You can undo it,** but choose carefully.
- Changes reach the sites within a minute.

## 3. If something goes wrong

| On screen                                                    | What to do                                                                 |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| アクセス用URLが正しくありません。 (Invalid access URL.)      | Open the URL you were given again.                                         |
| 読み込み / 送信 に失敗しました。 (Loading / sending failed.) | Check your signal and reload the page.                                     |
| 文化祭時間外です。 (Outside festival hours.)                 | You can send during festival hours, or during a test.                      |
| 受付対象外です。 (Not accepting.)                            | Only food-selling and cooking groups can send. Check with the head office. |
